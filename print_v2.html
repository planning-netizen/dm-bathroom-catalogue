Quality Assurance Bible | Section 3

Technical Trade Standards & Craftsmanship Manual

The Craftsmanship Benchmarks Behind the Turnkey White Glove Standard

2.1 Schluter Trim & Transition Standard

Mandatory Metal Edging: All exposed tile edges (niches, wall terminations, window jambs, bench tops) MUST use Schluter metal trim profiles color-matched to plumbing fixtures.
45° Miter Cuts: All 90° outside corners must be cleanly mitered at 45°. Trim must sit flush with tile without grout gaps.

2.2 Shower Door 78-Inch Height Standard

Standard Opening Height: 78 Inches is the official DMHI company standard height for all shower glass doors. Verified across Matte Black, Brushed Nickel, and Gold finishes to maintain strict collection consistency.

2.3 Comprehensive 4-Surface Painting

Non-Negotiable Scope: Every painted room package includes 4 distinct surfaces: Walls (moisture-resistant satin/eggshell), Trim/Baseboards (semi-gloss), Doors (durable enamel), and Ceiling (flat with mold inhibitor).

2.4 Material Care & Post-Job Turnover

Handling: Vanities stored in conditioned space; never damp garages. Turnover: Subcontractor rough-cleans daily; followed by our professional deep-cleaning team to sanitize fixtures, wipe tile dust, and polish glass before walkthrough.

Category PASS Criteria (White Glove Standard) FAIL Criteria (Requires Immediate Rework)
Tile & Schluter Tile level within 1/32"; Schluter profile mitered neatly at 45°; grout uniform. Lippage > 1/16"; raw tile edge exposed; Schluter trim missing or mismatched color.
Plumbing & Trim Fixtures installed level, centered, silicone sealed; zero leaks under pressure test. Escutcheons loose; fixture color mismatch; visible silicone smears.
Shower Glass 78" glass plumb within 1/16"; door swings freely; magnetic seal watertight. Glass door binding; water leaking onto floor; unapproved door height.
