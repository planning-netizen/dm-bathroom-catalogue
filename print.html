Answers to Common Questions

Will my home become a construction zone?

We understand that we're guests in your home. Our team takes great care to protect surrounding areas, maintain an organized workspace, and clean up daily.

How long will my bathroom remodel take?

Every project is unique, but before construction begins, we'll provide a clear project timeline and keep you informed throughout the remodeling process.

Do I need to be home during construction?

Not necessarily. Many homeowners continue with their normal routines while we complete their remodel, and we communicate regularly so you're always informed.

Can I personalize my bathroom design?

Absolutely. Every collection is designed to provide a professionally coordinated foundation while allowing you to personalize select finishes.
