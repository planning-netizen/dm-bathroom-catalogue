Technical Trade Standards & Craftsmanship Manual

The Craftsmanship Benchmarks Required of All Trade Crews

2.1 Schluter Trim & Transition Standard

Mandatory Metal Edging: All exposed tile edges (niches, wall terminations, window jambs) MUST use Schluter metal trim profiles color-matched to plumbing hardware.
45° Miter Cuts: Outside corners must be mitered cleanly at 45°.

2.2 Shower Door 78-Inch Height Standard

Standard Opening Height: 78 Inches is the official DMHI company standard height for all shower glass doors across Matte Black, Brushed Nickel, and Gold finishes.

2.3 Comprehensive 4-Surface Painting

Non-Negotiable Scope: Includes 4 distinct surfaces: Walls (moisture-resistant satin), Trim/Baseboards (semi-gloss), Doors (durable enamel), and Ceiling (flat with mold inhibitor).

2.4 Post-Job Turnover & Deep Cleaning

White Glove Turnover: Daily rough-cleans followed by our professional deep-cleaning team to sanitize fixtures, wipe tile dust, and polish glass before final walkthrough.
